# Corporate

The morning coffee is a pretty big part of the corporate lifestyle, a small positive way to start that day. I have one every morning myself.

I limit myself to one per day, but I've seen some real troopers go for 3+ a day. Really keeping the economy going with that level of spending.

# The DIY home special

Part of being an Australian is thinking that you can do everything better yourself, at home. So that's what I did with my coffee. Loaded up the internet and ordered myself a coffee machine (Breville Bambino Plus) and an expensive (for my standards) coffee grinder (Lagom Casa 65).

Australia has a really strong coffee culture, so sourcing specialty coffee beans from local roasters is really easy.

While writing all of this, I realised this little hobby of mine might be that mid 20s life crisis that I've heard so much about. Damn...

# The experience

So far it's been really enjoyable. I've tried three different beans to date, each from different roasters, and I get it now. 

I'm not enough of a connoisseur to start dishing out reviews, so don't expect those.
