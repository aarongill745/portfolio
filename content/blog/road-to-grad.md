# Introduction 

I've seen a lot of people ask what they should do if they have no internships, especially those studying CS, who are really in the thick of it.

---

## The secret sauce

Know which companies you actually have a good chance at getting into. 
Without an internship, some companies are nothing but a shot in the dark.

HFT? Pretty much no chance, sorry.

Big tech (Atlassian, Canva, Google, AWS, Microsoft) don't hire a lot of grads outside of returning interns. 
There's usually < 10 spots, and you're competing with the whales. 
Drawn out and crazy interview loops means your spending a tonne of time preparing for leetcode, system design, low level design, behaviourals. 
It's worth a shot, but this isn't where you need to be looking.

The answer? 

Big companies with signficant Australian Bases (Think Big banks, Telcos, Big 4 Consulting, Mastercard, etc). 
They've got have massive grad cohorts (sometimes > 300), relaxed requirements, and a big focus on the behaviourals instead of the technicals.

---

## The behavioural, you say?

It's all about culture fit. Two words, company values.

Here's what you do.

Step 1: Google "<company name> about us", read and remember everything that you see on that page.

Step 2: Slam anything you read on that page into your STAR respones. Remember, culture fit.

Step 3: Profit?

## Some resources that I really liked

Good resources are really hard to come by, so it's important to take note when you find some.

Resume: I used this [template](https://www.overleaf.com/latex/templates/jakes-resume/syzfjbzwjncs) (It's Latex).

Technical interviews: I Started with the [neetcode 150](https://neetcode.io/). It's honestly enough for most people, but you can find more on [leetcode](https://leetcode.com/).

Behaviourals: I thought this [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CAda15Tawlg) from [Hello Interview](https://www.hellointerview.com/) was really helpful on what to actually include in my answers.

System design: Not to sound like a shill, but [Hello Interview](https://www.hellointerview.com/) was the best resource I found for this, I.

Discussion forums: [Whirlpool](https://forums.whirlpool.net.au/forum/136) and [this subreddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/cscareerquestionsOCE/)
